# CFLC Product Master Template

## Purpose

This document is the working source of truth for Smart Steel's CFLC product range.

It is designed to support:

- website product pages
- CRM product setup
- quoting and estimate consistency
- retailer and stockist conversations
- internal operations and fulfilment planning

The goal is simple: the website, the quote, the sales conversation, and the retailer pack should all describe the same product in the same way.

## Current Smart Steel Structure

Use the current website structure as the commercial frame:

- `Projects & Systems`
  - custom work
  - broader warehouse/system enquiries
  - LSF-led custom route
- `Products / DIY Systems`
  - repeatable kits
  - product-style browsing
  - CFLC-led DIY route

Within `Products / DIY Systems`, CFLC is the lead system.

## Product Family Structure

Start with these CFLC product families:

1. `CFLC Carport Kits`
2. `CFLC DIY Warehouse Kits`

Recommended launch spans:

- `3m span`
- `6m span`
- `10m span`
- `12m span`

Recommended family split:

- `3m span`:
  - best first fit for single carports, narrow side covers, and compact utility covers
- `6m span`:
  - smaller storage, workshop, and utility structures
- `10m span`:
  - mid-range warehouse and covered work-area kits
- `12m span`:
  - larger warehouse and agricultural/storage kits

## Naming Rules

Keep naming disciplined and client-friendly.

Use:

- `CFLC Single Carport Kit`
- `CFLC Double Carport Kit`
- `CFLC 6m Span Warehouse Kit`
- `CFLC 10m Span Warehouse Kit`
- `CFLC 12m Span Warehouse Kit`

Avoid:

- internal spreadsheet naming
- unclear internal nicknames
- inconsistent mixed `LCSS` / `CFLC` wording

Client-facing wording should use `CFLC`.
